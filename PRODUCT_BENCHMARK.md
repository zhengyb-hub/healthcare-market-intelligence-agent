# Product Benchmark Notes

## Objective

The 1.1 product pass benchmarks workflow patterns from established competitive and market intelligence products. The goal is not feature parity. It is to make the existing evidence, traceability, and review foundation easier to operate as a daily intelligence workspace.

## Reference patterns

| Platform | Product pattern | Applied in this project |
|---|---|---|
| [Klue](https://klue.com/competitive-intelligence-software) | Centralized, continuously updated competitive intelligence and reusable battlecards | Structured competitor, executive, and sales outputs continue to reuse one reviewed Claim and Evidence basis |
| [Crayon](https://www.crayon.co/product/aggregate) | Capture, prioritize, and distribute intelligence with feedback loops | The overview routes material signals into evidence inspection, review, comparison, and controlled outputs |
| [AlphaSense](https://www.alpha-sense.com/solutions/market-intelligence-platform/) | Custom dashboards, monitoring, alerts, and collaboration around trusted research | Program-health indicators and priority-first signal views make freshness and unresolved work visible |
| [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/what-we-offer/platform/) | Searchable company intelligence, market visualizations, and decision-oriented shortlists | Vendor search, market map, comparison scope, and evidence-linked opportunity hypotheses form one analysis flow |
| [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/corp/web/competitive-analysis/) | Competitive trackers, alerts, and at-a-glance benchmarking | Watchlist health, signal priority, filterable views, and coverage comparisons are surfaced consistently |
| [Kompyte](https://www.kompyte.com/) | Automated monitoring with noise reduction and always-current enablement | Feed search, priority sorting, advanced filters, and evidence-link counts reduce activity-feed noise |

## Implemented decisions

### 1. Analyst overview over activity dashboard

The Overview leads with decision readiness and priority queues. Coverage charts remain available, but they no longer displace work that requires attention.

### 2. Priority before chronology

The intelligence feed supports a dedicated high-priority view plus priority-first, newest-first, and oldest-first ordering. Search works across Vendor, title, summary, event type, and source type. Watchlist-only and event-specific views reduce irrelevant signals.

### 3. Restraint over decoration

The interface uses a flat workspace canvas, compact page headers, limited shadows, square status labels, and dense information rows. Decorative gradients, floating-card movement, and generic marketing language are intentionally avoided.

### 4. Trust is visible in every workflow

Evidence validation, Brief review coverage, reviewed-Claim traceability, and watchlist freshness use consistent progress components. These indicators remain explicitly classified as synthetic evaluation metrics.

### 5. One evidence basis, multiple outputs

Comparison, Executive Brief, and Sales Enablement views continue to reuse stored Claims and Evidence instead of generating disconnected narratives.

### 6. Human review is a first-class product surface

The Review workspace now communicates triage order and risk. Unsupported Claims remain the highest-risk queue, while Brief and Evidence decisions preserve their distinct workflows.

## Boundaries

- No external crawling, CRM integration, or production alert delivery was added.
- No synthetic metric is presented as production impact.
- No vendor score or market-share estimate is invented.
- The existing deterministic demo and Evidence Pack rules remain authoritative.
